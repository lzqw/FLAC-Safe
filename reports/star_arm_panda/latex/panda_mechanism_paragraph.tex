To visualize the audit mechanism in an embodied control setting, we include a Panda obstacle-reaching case study. The state in Figure~X is selected by a fixed audit criterion: the actor mean is predicted safe, the reference-to-current corridor contains a high-risk shadow, the Corridor Risk Lift is positive, and the risky shadows are not executed. This case directly instantiates the pointwise blind spot in a robot-arm scene. The selected STAR+Exec episode reaches the target with zero keep-out cost, but broader Panda evaluations remain mixed; we therefore use this result as a qualitative mechanism visualization rather than a standalone benchmark claim.
